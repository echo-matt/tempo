Patterns
Home renders sixteen sections and they are not all the same shape. There are three list patterns plus two one-offs, and every section is one of them. Designing a single carousel and assuming the rest would follow is what went wrong the first time.

The line above a heading is not always decoration. “Last week” opens a time filter, and the equivalents on the podcast and radio tabs add a feed. Those take the accent so they read as controls; the two that really are subtitles stay grey.

Most played, Last played, Recently added, Made for you, Best of, Radio stations. Albums are square at 148dp with an 8dp radius; artists are circles at 132dp, since a circle reads larger than a square of the same width. Playlists is the odd one out: it uses the same rows as pattern B but stacked vertically, not paged.

One-offs

2000
Held Note
Flashback year tiles and the Jump back in grid. The year tile keeps its accent spine but drops the elevation, so it sits on the same flat surface as everything else. The grid is two columns of the recently-played head — it shares that feed rather than fetching its own, so it is as long as your listening history allows, not always six.
